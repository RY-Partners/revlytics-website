FAQ

Common questions

How is Revlytics different from my existing CRM or SFA?+

Revlytics isn't built to replace Salesforce or HubSpot. Traditional CRM/SFA focuses on recording and managing activity. Revlytics analyzes the data you already have and actively tells reps who to contact and what to offer — freeing up time for actual customer conversations instead of deciding where to start.

Does the AI ever contact a customer automatically?+

No. The rep always makes the final call and takes the final action. The AI analyzes data and recommends a next step and channel — sending an email or making a call is always a human decision. This "human-in-the-loop" principle is core to the product, not a limitation.

Can we use Revlytics if our customer data is a mess?+

Yes — business cards, spreadsheets, and inconsistent legacy lists all work. Onboarding includes data-cleansing and digitization support so messy source data isn't a blocker to getting started.

Do we need a CRM already in place?+

No. Revlytics syncs bidirectionally with major CRMs where you have one, but works as a standalone workspace if you don't — recording and organizing sales activity as your system of record from day one.

What kind of company is this built for?+

Mid-market and SMB companies where sales resources are stretched thin — especially those wanting to mine dormant customers, put old business-card/contact data to work, reduce dependence on individual reps' tribal knowledge, or simply cut daily CRM input burden.

What data does the AI actually analyze?+

Contract status, deal/negotiation history, communication history, and (where available) web activity — combined to surface who's worth prioritizing right now and what would resonate with them.

Does Revlytics integrate with tools we already use?+

Yes — major CRMs, email systems, and phone/call infrastructure are all supported integration targets. Specific connectors are confirmed during onboarding.
