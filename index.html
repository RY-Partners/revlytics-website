The Problem

Your best sales opportunities are buried in spreadsheets and business cards

Three forces compound the problem for Japan's mid-market sales teams.

📉

A shrinking sales workforce

Fewer reps are covering more accounts than ever, with no time left to prospect dormant or past customers.

🗄️

CRM/SFA that nobody uses

Built for management reporting, not the rep's morning — so data entry feels like a tax, and adoption erodes.

🧠

Tribal knowledge instead of process

Prioritization and pitch content live in individual reps' heads. When they leave, the knowledge leaves too.

We're not another CRM. We're the screen your reps open first.

Every CRM will have "AI" soon. Revlytics wins by owning the first five minutes of a rep's day — the moment that decides where their time goes.

How It Works

From login to logged activity, in one flow

1

Open Revlytics, see today's priorities

The Home screen shows the accounts worth contacting today, why, and what to say — before you touch your CRM or inbox.

2

Ask the AI, decide, act

The persistent chat assistant already knows the context of whatever you're looking at. You ask, review, edit — and reach out by whatever channel makes sense.

3

It logs itself and syncs

Voice notes, calls, and emails become activity records automatically, and sync back to your existing CRM — no separate data-entry pass.
